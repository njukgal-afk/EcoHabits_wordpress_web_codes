Why we built Eco Habit

Eco Habit started with a simple idea: real change doesn't come from guilt or grand gestures, it comes from small choices repeated often enough to matter. We built this platform to help people get more aware of sustainability and gently shift their daily habits - one small step at a time, toward a more sustainable future.
